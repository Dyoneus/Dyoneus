## Hey, I'm Jia Yuan.

Computer games got me interested in technology. Learning what happens behind the screen kept me interested.

I went from studying systems and networking at ITE to IT at Ngee Ann Polytechnic. Now, as a Year 1 Information Security undergraduate at SIT, I'm learning how to protect the systems I grew curious about. Along the way, I've worked on automation at the Ministry of Education and built a team project around giving electronics a second life.

I'm interested in **SOC Analyst and Security Analyst roles**, and I'm building the knowledge to get there.
